# Adidas-US-Sales-PowerBi-Dashboard-
Analysis of Adidas dataset to breakdown the sales and profit margins from different regions and states in the US and offer insights to improve sales perfomance for maximum profitability

## Business Problem

Adidas would like to get key business insights on:
1. Sales dynamics and performance drivers
   
2. Identification of geographical areas with high and low sales potential
   
3. Informed pricing and margin strategies for improved profitability
   
4. Actionable recommendations for optimizing sales and profit


## Data Overview
Adidas dataset contains 9649 rows and 13 variables. The time period of the dataset is for two years 2020 and 2021.

#### Key variables

> Retailer

> Region

> State

> City

> Product

> Units sold

> Sales method


## Business Questions:

1. Which State has the most sales?
   
2. Which region has the most sales?
 
3. Which are the best and worst perfoming months by sales?
 
4. Who is the best retailer by sales?
 
5. Which is the bst selling product?
